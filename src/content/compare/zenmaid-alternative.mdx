Most comparison pages on this site open by pointing out that the other tool was built for
plumbers. That argument doesn't work here.

ZenMaid was built for maid services specifically, by people who know the trade. It schedules
well, it's been around a long time, and its entry plan costs less than ours. If you're looking
for a page that tells you they're bad software, this isn't it.

Here's the honest version of where each one fits.

## What it costs

ZenMaid's pricing page shows monthly rates by default. As listed on 27 July 2026:

| Plan | Price | Appointment limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $19/mo | Up to 40 appointments per month |
| Pro | $39/mo | Unlimited |
| Pro Max | $49/mo | Unlimited |

Against CleanDock at $29.99 / $49.99 / $99.99, the headline is simple: **their entry plan is
cheaper than ours.** $19 against $29.99. We're not going to argue with arithmetic.

### But check the appointment cap before you compare those two numbers

Starter is capped at **40 appointments a month**. A solo cleaner doing three jobs a day hits that
in under three weeks. Two cleans a day and you're over it before month-end.

For most working cleaning businesses, the real comparison isn't Starter — it's **Pro at $39/mo**,
which is where unlimited appointments start.

| | CleanDock | ZenMaid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest plan | $29.99/mo | $19/mo (40 appointments) |
| Cheapest unlimited-job plan | **$29.99/mo** | $39/mo (Pro) |

So if you're busy enough that a 40-job ceiling would bother you, CleanDock Single is roughly ten
dollars a month cheaper than the ZenMaid plan you'd actually need. If you're doing fewer than 40
cleans a month, Starter is cheaper and we'd rather you knew that.

## Where the two products actually differ

The pricing difference is small. The shape difference is not.

**ZenMaid is organised around the schedule.** Its tiers step up through scheduling views,
dispatch, booking forms, payroll and PTO tracking. That's a coherent product for a business whose
main problem is who-is-where-tomorrow.

**CleanDock is organised around the job's whole lifecycle** — quote, schedule, proof, invoice,
paid. The scheduling is there and it's good, but it's one step in a chain rather than the centre
of the product.

Which matters depends entirely on where your time actually goes.

### Quoting, and the one-tap handoff to invoice

You build a branded quote before you're out of the driveway. The client approves it. **One tap
turns that quote into an invoice** — same line items, no re-keying — and they pay by card or ACH
from the email.

If you quote a lot of one-off work — move-outs, deep cleans, post-construction — that loop is
most of the job. If you're almost entirely recurring residential and rarely quote, it matters
less.

### Before-and-after photos attached to the job

Cleaners shoot before and after photos from their phone. The photos attach to that job
permanently, next to the tracked time.

"You missed a room" is the most common complaint in this trade. Photos end that conversation
rather than extending it, and for move-outs and Airbnb turnovers — where a deposit or a review is
at stake — they're often the reason you get rebooked.

### People aren't a line item

Single covers one person. Team covers five at $49.99. Team+ removes the cap entirely at $99.99.
Adding a part-timer for the summer doesn't change your bill.

## Where ZenMaid is the better choice

- **You're under 40 cleans a month and want the cheapest thing that works.** Starter at $19 is
  cheaper than anything we offer, and it invoices and takes card payments.
- **Your problem is purely scheduling** — who's where, in what order, with what checklist.
- **You want a long track record.** They've been serving this specific trade for years and have
  the community to show for it.
- **Payroll inside the scheduler** matters to you.

## Where CleanDock is the better choice

- **You quote regularly** and want quote-to-invoice-to-paid without re-entering anything.
- **You want photo proof on every job**, not as an add-on.
- **You're growing a crew** and don't want per-head pricing.
- **You want one app** covering the job from first text to final payment.

## Common questions

**Is CleanDock cheaper than ZenMaid?**

Depends on the plan. Their Starter at $19 is cheaper than our Single at $29.99, but it caps you
at 40 appointments a month. Compared to ZenMaid Pro at $39 — the first plan with unlimited
appointments — CleanDock Single is about ten dollars a month cheaper. At crew size the gap widens,
because we don't price per user.

**Both are built for cleaning. What's the actual difference?**

Emphasis. ZenMaid's product is built outward from the schedule. Ours is built around the whole
job — quote, clean, photograph, invoice, get paid. Neither is wrong; they suit different
businesses.

**Can I take card payments?**

Yes — card and ACH, paid from the invoice email. QuickBooks sync is supported. ZenMaid also takes
card payments, so this isn't a differentiator either way.

**Does it work on a phone?**

That's the primary way it's used. Cleaners clock in, shoot photos and add notes from the jobsite.
