If you've searched for Launch27 recently and found something called Automaid, you read that
right. In May 2026 the company announced that **Launch27 is now Automaid**, operating under
Fullsteam.

Rebrands happen. But a rebrand that comes with a stated change in direction is a reasonable
moment to look around, which is presumably why you're here.

## What changed

Launch27's original pitch was booking-first: a prospective client enters details about their
home, gets instant pricing, and books with a card on file. That online-booking experience is what
the product was known for, and it grew from there into scheduling, payments and team management.

Automaid's stated direction is heavier automation: in the company's own framing, software where
owners log in to see how the business did rather than to run it.

That's a real vision and it will suit plenty of operators. If you want your business to run
itself and you trust software to make the calls, you should take a serious look at it.

If you'd rather keep your hands on the wheel, approving bookings, adjusting quotes and deciding
which jobs are worth taking, that's a different product, and this page is about that.

## Should you switch at all?

A rebrand on its own isn't a reason to move. Migrating software costs you a weekend and some
risk, and "my vendor changed its logo" doesn't repay that.

Worth moving if:

- The automation-first direction isn't how you want to run your business.
- Your pricing or terms changed in a way you didn't choose.
- You were already frustrated and the rebrand is just what made you look.

Worth staying if:

- It works and your clients are used to your booking flow.
- Automated everything is genuinely what you wanted.
