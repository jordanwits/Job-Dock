Turnover cleaning runs on a clock nobody else in this trade deals with. Checkout at 11, check-in
at 3, and a guest who paid for a spotless room either walks into one or leaves a review about it.

That's a different job from a biweekly residential clean, and most cleaning software is built for
the biweekly.

## Photos are the product

For a regular house-cleaning client, before-and-after photos settle the occasional argument. For a
short-term rental host, they're the reason you keep the contract.

Your cleaners shoot before and after photos from their phone at the property. Every photo attaches
to that specific turnover, permanently, next to the tracked time. When a host asks whether the
unit was ready, or a guest claims it wasn't, you have the timestamped answer instead of a
recollection.

Hosts managing several units aren't being difficult when they ask. They're managing their own
review score, and the cleaner who can prove the work is the cleaner who keeps getting the
properties.

## Same-day turnarounds, on one calendar

Turnovers cluster. Sunday and Monday are a wall, midweek is quiet, and a late cancellation
reshuffles everything.

- Drag to reschedule when a checkout slips
- See every cleaner at a glance so two people don't get sent to the same unit
- Recurring blocks for the properties you clean on a fixed pattern

If a host adds a property, you set it up once and it repeats.

## Billing hosts who own several units

Property managers and multi-unit hosts don't want an invoice per turnover arriving one at a time.
Build the invoice with the line items you actually did, send it, and they pay by card or ACH from
the email.

For a one-off turnover, a quote goes out from your phone and converts to an invoice in one tap
when they say yes.

## Booking requests without the 9pm texts

Put a booking link in your listing, your Instagram bio, or the message you send new hosts.
Requests come in around the clock, land in front of you for approval, and drop onto the calendar
once you accept.

You're not negotiating Thursday availability over text after dinner.
